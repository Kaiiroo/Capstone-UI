Data Privacy Notice

PRESCRIBE-AID handles prescriptions, which contain patients' health information. Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), health information is sensitive personal information. Every user must read and agree to this notice before using the system.

How your data and your patients' data are protected

  • Only authorized staff. Records can be opened only by signed-in GuadaHealth accounts. The database checks every request, so no one can view records without signing in.
  • Encrypted. Data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and stored in a secured cloud database (Supabase).
  • Every action is recorded. Uploads, edits, verifications, and exports are logged with the account and time, so any access can be traced.
  • Used only for record keeping. Information is used to transcribe, verify, and keep prescription records. It is never sold or shared for marketing.

What is collected and who processes it

  • Collected: prescription images, their recognized text, the medicines, dosages, and diagnoses written on them, the prescribing doctor's name, and your account email.
  • Processed by: Supabase (sign-in and storage), Roboflow (runs the text recognition), and Anthropic (its Claude model reads the text from uploaded images). Images are sent to Roboflow and Anthropic only to read the text.
  • Kept: for as long as GuadaHealth needs the records, following its records retention policy.

Your rights under the Data Privacy Act

You and your patients have the right to be informed, to access, to correct, to object, to have data erased or blocked, to data portability, and to be indemnified for damages, and the right to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. To use these rights, contact GuadaHealth's Data Protection Officer through the clinic administration.

Your responsibilities as a user

Upload only prescriptions you are authorized to handle, and do not share, download, or copy patient information outside of your work.

You cannot use PRESCRIBE-AID without agreeing. You have been signed out.
